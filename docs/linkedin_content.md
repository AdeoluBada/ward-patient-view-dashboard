## Project description
I developed a Ward Patient View dashboard concept to explore how structured data and accessible reporting can improve the visibility of changes recorded during shift handovers. The original Excel project brings together a patient roster, shift handover log and a colour-coded dashboard to support review of positive, negative and neutral changes. It demonstrates how a consistent reporting structure can help staff identify items for further attention and understand activity across shifts.

For my public analytics portfolio, I translated the concept into an interactive browser-based demonstration using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Chart.js. Visitors can filter synthetic handover records by shift, observation category and change type, then explore KPI cards, reporting distributions and the latest-record view. The demonstration intentionally excludes the original workbook and all patient or staff information.

This project showcases my approach to Excel dashboard design, analytical logic, performance reporting, data presentation and information governance. It is an illustrative portfolio project rather than a live clinical system, and no operational impact or patient outcome improvement is claimed.
