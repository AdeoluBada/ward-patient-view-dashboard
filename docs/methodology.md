# Methodology

1. Inspected the source workbook's four worksheet structures and handover fields.
2. Identified personal identifiers, staff assignments, mental health legal status, and clinical narratives as unsafe to publish.
3. Generated a wholly synthetic, separate demonstration dataset. No real/source patient or staff records were copied.
4. Recreated the dashboard concept using HTML/CSS/JavaScript and Chart.js with client-side filters.
5. Defined each KPI in [KPI definitions](kpi_definitions.md).
6. Kept the original workbook offline and excluded it from the repository.

The synthetic records are not representative of ward performance and should not be used to infer operational or clinical outcomes. The demonstration preserves the conceptual colour coding and reporting dimensions, not a pixel-perfect reproduction of Excel's interface.
